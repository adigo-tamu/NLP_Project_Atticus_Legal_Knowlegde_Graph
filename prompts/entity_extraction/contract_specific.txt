You are analyzing a LEGAL CONTRACT. Pay special attention to:

CONTRACT-SPECIFIC ENTITY PATTERNS:

1. **Party Identification:**
   - Look for signature blocks, preambles, and defined party terms
   - Track aliases: "Company", "Seller", "Licensor", "such party"
   - Extract party addresses and contact information as attributes

2. **Temporal Patterns in Contracts:**
   - Effective dates: "effective as of", "entered into on"
   - Term lengths: "for a period of", "term of X years"
   - Deadlines: "within X days", "no later than", "by [date]"
   - Renewal periods: "automatically renew", "renewal term"
   - Notice periods: "X days prior notice"

3. **Financial Terms in Contracts:**
   - License fees, royalties, service fees
   - Payment schedules: "payable in installments", "quarterly payments"
   - Late fees and interest rates
   - Caps and floors: "not to exceed", "minimum of"
   - Currency conversions and adjustments

4. **Obligations - Common Patterns:**
   - "shall", "must", "will", "is required to", "agrees to"
   - Negative obligations: "shall not", "may not", "agrees not to"
   - Conditional obligations: "shall... if", "must... unless"
   - Ongoing vs. one-time obligations

5. **Rights - Common Patterns:**
   - "may", "has the right to", "is entitled to", "is permitted to"
   - Exclusive vs. non-exclusive rights
   - Transferable vs. non-transferable rights
   - Rights subject to conditions

6. **Legal Concepts - Contract-Specific:**
   - Indemnification provisions
   - Confidentiality obligations
   - Intellectual property definitions
   - Force majeure events
   - Material breach standards
   - Good faith requirements

7. **Cross-References:**
   - "as defined in Section X"
   - "pursuant to Article Y"
   - "subject to the terms of Section Z"
   - Exhibits and schedules: "Exhibit A", "Schedule 1"

8. **Defined Terms:**
   - Usually capitalized mid-sentence
   - Often in parentheses: "Software (as defined herein)"
   - Extract both the term and its definition if present

SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS:

- If a party is referred to by multiple names (e.g., "ABC Corp", "Company", "Seller"), extract each mention
- For obligations, try to identify WHO has the obligation
- For rights, try to identify WHO holds the right
- For temporal entities, normalize if possible (e.g., "thirty days" → "30 days")
- For financial terms, extract numerical values when present
- Mark uncertain extractions with lower confidence scores

ANTI-PATTERNS (DO NOT EXTRACT):

- Generic procedural words: "herein", "thereof", "hereunder" (unless part of a larger entity)
- Structural markers: "whereas", "therefore", "notwithstanding"
- Article/section numbers without context (extract as Clause_Reference only when referenced)

Focus on SUBSTANTIVE legal entities that convey meaning about the contract's terms, parties, and obligations.
